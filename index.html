About SAE IIITDM

The Society of Automotive Engineers (SAE) collegiate chapter at IIITDM Kurnool is a student-run organization that bridges the gap between classroom learning and real-world engineering challenges.

We participate in national and international competitions, organize workshops, and collaborate on projects that push the boundaries of automotive engineering. Our members gain hands-on experience in design, manufacturing, and innovation.
